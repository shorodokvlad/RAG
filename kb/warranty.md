# Warranty

## Do products have a warranty?
Yes. Every product sold by SHV Store has the legal guarantee of conformity of 2 years from
delivery, as required in the European Union. Some manufacturers add their own commercial
warranty on top; check the product's specifications for its warranty details.

## What does the warranty cover?
Defects and faults that weren't caused by misuse, accidents or unauthorized repairs. It
doesn't cover normal wear, or damage such as drops or liquid damage.

## How do I make a warranty claim?
Contact customer support with your order number and a short description of the problem.
Support will tell you how to send the product for repair or replacement.
