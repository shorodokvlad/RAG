# Ordering and your account

## Do I need an account to order?
You can browse products and add them to your cart without an account. To place an order
you need to sign in or create a free account; the cart asks you to sign in at checkout.

## How do I place an order?
Add products to your cart (choose the color, storage or other option first when a product
has several), open the cart, check the delivery estimate for your address and press
"Place order".

## Where can I see my orders?
Signed-in customers see all their orders and each item's status in their profile, under
My Orders.

## What do the order statuses mean?
- Pending: the order was received and is waiting for confirmation.
- Confirmed: the order was accepted and is being prepared.
- Shipped: the order left the warehouse and is on its way.
- Delivered: the courier delivered the order.
- Cancelled: the order or item was cancelled and won't be shipped.
- Returned: the item was returned to the store.

## Can I save products for later?
Yes. Use the heart button on a product to add it to your Favorites. Open them with the
heart icon at the top of the page.
