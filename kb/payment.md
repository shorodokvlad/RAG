# Prices and payment

## Which currency are prices in?
All prices on SHV Store are in euros (€).

## Which payment methods do you accept?
SHV Store accepts Visa and MasterCard cards.

## Is the price shown the final price?
The product price is shown on the product page and the cart totals your items. Delivery
fees, when they apply, depend on your address and are shown separately in the delivery
estimate.
