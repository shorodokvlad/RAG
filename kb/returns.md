# Returns and cancellations

## Can I return a product?
Yes. As SHV Store sells online in the European Union, you can withdraw from a purchase
within 14 days of receiving the product, without giving a reason. Contact customer
support with your order number to start a return.

## In what condition must a returned product be?
Return the product complete, with its accessories and packaging. You may check the
product as you would in a shop; if it was used beyond that, its value may be reduced.

## When do I get my money back?
After the return is approved, the refund is made within 14 days, to the payment method
used for the order.

## Can I cancel an order?
Contact customer support as soon as possible with your order number. An order that
hasn't shipped yet can be cancelled; once it has shipped, return it instead.
