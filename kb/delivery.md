# Delivery and shipping

## Where do you deliver?
SHV Store delivers across Romania (all counties) and to countries in the European Union.

## What delivery options are there?
Two options: Standard Delivery and Express Delivery (faster). The price and the
estimated delivery dates of both are shown on the product page and in the cart for your
delivery address.

## How much does delivery cost?
The delivery fee depends on the destination (country, and county within Romania) and on
the option you choose. Standard Delivery is free when the order subtotal reaches the free
delivery threshold for your destination. The product page and the cart show the exact fee
and threshold for your address.

## How long does delivery take?
Orders are prepared and handed to the courier within about 2 business days. Transit time
then depends on your destination and on Standard or Express delivery. The product page
and the cart show an estimated delivery date range for your address.

## Do you deliver on weekends and holidays?
Couriers don't deliver on Sundays or on Romanian public holidays; delivery estimates
already skip those days.
